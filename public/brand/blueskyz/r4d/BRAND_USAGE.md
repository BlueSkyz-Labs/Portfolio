# R4d Brand Usage — Production Master Candidate

## Core identity

Public-name target: **BlueSkyz Labs**

Core palette:

- Ink
- Porcelain
- Cobalt

Verdigris is a **system-context accent**, not a master-mark color.
Gold is not part of the R4d core identity.

## Mark hierarchy

- Full mark: 32 px and above by default.
- Micro mark: 16–24 px and other constrained icon contexts.
- Monochrome capability is mandatory.
- `symbol_material_expression.svg` is high-expression support only; it must not become the only recognizable form.

## Wordmark

The wordmark is outlined and has no runtime font dependency.
Its `Z` is a custom candidate glyph derived from R4d's latent-diagonal grammar.

## Product attribution

Preferred: **A BlueSkyz Labs product**
Compact: **by BlueSkyz Labs**
Engineering provenance: **Built by BlueSkyz Labs**

Do not place the parent logo on every product screen. Use attribution where portfolio context is otherwise unclear.

## Prohibited drift

- no gold/metallic luxury skin
- no glow/shimmer as default identity
- no literal lotus/bird/wing/cloud/AI motifs
- no ad-hoc colorways
- no re-drawing or distorting the master mark
- no `®` unless applicable registration exists

## Evidence truth

`Distinctive`, `ownable`, or `proprietary` in design rationale do not establish legal rights or audience recognition.
Those remain separate legal/E5/E6 gates.
